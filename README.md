# Studying concerts, shaping concerts

Slides for the keynote at NeuroMusic 2026, McMaster University, Hamilton, 21 November 2026, by Alexander Refsum Jensenius (University of Oslo).

Open the slides: https://alexarje.github.io/studying-concerts-shaping-concerts/

Keys: arrows to move, `s` for speaker notes, `o` for an overview, `?light` in the address for a light background.

Figures and photographs are from the fourMs Lab, RITMO, MusicLab and Bodies in Concert, and from the open-access articles cited on the slides. Two videos are embedded from YouTube.
